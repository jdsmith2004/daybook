Quit porn without shame.

Daybook is a free, private companion for quitting porn, or another habit. Log urges and slips in a few taps, learn your pattern, and keep going after a hard day. A slip is data, not a reset: what you build stays yours.

Why Daybook is different

People do not fail because they slip. They fail when they lose hope. Most tools are built around a streak that drops to zero, so day 89 can feel like losing everything. Daybook is built the other way around.

Progress that is never lost

Points, levels, badges and the days you built stay earned, even after a slip. Daybook counts what you kept, not only your latest run.

Help in the hard minute

SOS opens with a slow breath, then offers a tool that fits the moment: grounding, a 10-minute pause, moving, your own reasons, or a text to someone you trust.

Learn your own pattern

Daybook shows the hours and moments that are hardest for you, from your own log, so you can plan ahead for them.

Keep going after a slip

Rekindle is a short, honest look back and one small step forward. Its quest can keep your run.

Private by design

No ads, no tracking, nothing sold. No account needed. Face ID lock, and a plain name and icon on your home screen.

Free for everyone

Nothing to unlock and no subscription. If it helps you, you can leave a tip; a tip changes nothing in the app.

What is inside

  • SOS: breathe first, then a tool that fits the moment.
  • Logging: an urge or a slip in a few taps, with an optional note.
  • Your pattern: your hardest hours and days, and a heads-up before them.
  • Rekindle: after a slip, a kind look back and a small quest.
  • Lessons: built on good research about habits and urges, written plainly and with hope.
  • Your plan: why you are doing this, and what you will do when an urge comes.
  • Daily rhythm: a morning check, an evening wind-down, and a weekly review.
  • Graduation: a permanent award at 90 days with no slip.

Common questions

What is a good app to help me quit porn?

Daybook is built for exactly that, without shame. It helps in the moment of an urge, shows the hours that are hardest for you, and helps you keep going after a slip instead of starting over. It is free, private, and works with no account.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything is free for everyone, with no ads, no subscription and nothing behind a paywall.

Do I need an account?

No. SOS, logging, Rekindle, your plan, the tools and Today all work with no account, and your log stays on your phone. A free account keeps a safe copy, syncs your phone and the web, and opens the lessons after the first three.

What happens after a slip?

A slip is data, not a reset. Your points, levels, badges and the days you built stay yours. Rekindle helps you look back honestly and take one small step.

Does it block websites?

No. Daybook is not a filter and does not watch what you do. It pairs well with a blocker if you want one.

Is it only for porn?

It is made first for quitting porn, and it works for other habits you want to change too.

Where can I get it?

Daybook works in any browser today. The iPhone and Android apps are in testing and come to the App Store and Google Play soon.

If you are in crisis

Daybook is not medical care. If you are thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 in the US, or call your local emergency number.
