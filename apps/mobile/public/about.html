How Daybook helps

  1. In the hard minute

    Tap SOS. Breathe first, then pick a tool that fits: grounding, a 10-minute pause, moving, your own reasons, or a text to someone you trust.

  2. Over the weeks

    Log urges and slips in a few taps. Daybook shows the hours and moments that are hardest for you, and gives you a heads-up before them.

  3. After a slip

    Rekindle is a short, honest look back and one small step forward. Its quest can keep your run.

Also inside: short lessons built on research about habits and urges, your own plan, an evening check-in and wind-down, and a weekly review. It works for other habits too.

What Daybook never does

  • Never shows ads, tracks you, or sells or shares what you log.
  • Never needs an account to help you. Without one, your log never leaves your phone.
  • Never takes away what you earned.
  • Never says what it is for on your home screen or in a reminder.
  • Never locks care behind a paywall. It is free, for everyone.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Daybook is free, with no ads and no subscription, and everything for hard moments is free for everyone. A tip of $5 or more opens sync, the safe copy and your whole pattern.

Who can see what I log?

Only you. Without an account, it stays on your phone. With an account, it is stored under your own account and sent only to you when you are signed in.

Do I need an account?

No. SOS, logging, Rekindle, your plan, the tools and Today all work with no account, ever. A free account opens the lessons after the first eight, and a tip of $5 or more opens sync and a safe copy of your log.

What happens after a slip?

A slip is data, not a reset. Your points, levels, badges and good days stay yours, and Rekindle helps you take one small step.

Does it block websites?

No. Daybook is not a filter and does not watch what you do. It pairs well with a blocker if you want one.

Is it only for porn?

It is made first for quitting porn, and it works for other habits you want to change too.

If you are in crisis
