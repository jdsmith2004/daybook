Bigger questions

Am I addicted to porn?

You do not have to settle the label first. Experts still debate what to call it. What counts is what it does to your life, and whether you want something different. Help is there whatever you call it.

What the experts say

The World Health Organization’s list of conditions includes compulsive sexual behaviour disorder: a long-lasting pattern of failing to control intense, repeated sexual urges, with marked distress or real problems in life. It is not specifically about porn. The main US psychiatric manual does not list porn addiction; a related idea, hypersexual disorder, was considered and left out because the evidence was not strong enough. The debate is about the name and the definition.

That is why many people use plain, neutral words like “problematic use” or “compulsive use.” Only a licensed professional can say what applies to you, and none of it changes what you can do today.

Two questions worth asking

The distress can come from two different places, and many people feel some of each:

  • Control: you cannot stop when you want to, and it takes more time, sleep or money than you mean it to.
  • Values: intense guilt, mainly because it clashes with what you believe.

One research team proposed that much of the feeling of being addicted may come from that second one, a gap between what you believe and what you do, which they call moral incongruence. The WHO draws a related line: distress from moral disapproval alone does not count toward its diagnosis. Both are real, neither means something is wrong with you, and each points to different help.

Help that works

A 2022 review of treatments for compulsive sexual behavior and problem porn use found that treatment appeared to improve symptoms, and that cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) had the strongest support. A therapist, a group, or one person you trust are all real places to start. There are free and low-cost routes too, like an employee assistance program (EAP) at work, Open Path, and community health centers.

One thing to do tonight

Write one sentence about what this habit costs you: sleep, focus, time, closeness, or how you feel about yourself. That sentence, in your own words, is a better guide than any label.
