In the hard minute

How do I stop a porn urge right now?

You do not have to fight it, and you do not have to give in. An urge is a wave: it rises, peaks and passes. Right now your only job is to outlast this one, and a few simple steps make the next ten minutes easier.

Right now, in this order

  1. Name it. Say to yourself, “This is an urge. It is a wave.” Naming it puts a small gap between the urge and what you do next.
  2. Breathe out slowly. Breathe in through your nose, sip in a little more air, then breathe out through your mouth, longer than the breath in. Keep going for about a minute.
  3. Move. Stand up and walk to another room, or do ten push-ups or squats. Move first, then decide.
  4. Come back to the room. Name 5 things you can see, 4 you can feel, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell and 1 you can taste. A sip of water counts.
  5. Give it ten minutes. Then notice what the urge did. Ten minutes is not a magic number. It is short enough to try.

Why this works

Psychologist Alan Marlatt called this urge surfing: notice the urge, breathe, and watch it rise and fall. College smokers who learned it had urges just as strong as everyone else’s, but over the next week they smoked fewer cigarettes. The urge did not shrink. What they did with it changed. That gap between the urge and the action is the skill, and it grows every time you use it.

An urge also runs on a picture in your head, and that picture uses the same space in your mind as real seeing and feeling. Give your eyes and hands something real, and the picture has less room. In a week-long study, three minutes of a block game after each craving lowered cravings, sexual urges among them, by about 14 points out of 100, and it kept working all week.

Your body helps too. A review of studies on cigarette cravings found strong evidence that a short bout of exercise eases a craving for a while. And a slow breath with a long breath out takes a minute, costs nothing, and settles the body while the wave passes.

One thing to do tonight

Practice the long breath out once tonight, while it is quiet: in through the nose, a little more air, then a long, slow breath out. One minute. When the next urge comes, you will know exactly where to find it.
