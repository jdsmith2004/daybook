Questions people ask while quitting porn

Plain, honest answers, built on research about habits and urges. Each one ends with one thing you can do tonight. A slip is data, not a reset, and you can always keep going.

In the hard minute

  • How do I stop a porn urge right now?An urge is a wave. Five steps for the next ten minutes.
  • Why do I relapse at night?Tired, alone, and the same room as last time.
  • How do I stop thinking about porn?A thought is not an order. Name it, and crowd out the picture.

After a slip

  • I relapsed. What do I do now?The next hour matters most: honest, kind, one small thing.
  • Does a relapse reset all my progress?No. The good days you built are still yours.
  • How do I stop one slip from turning into a binge?Step out of the snowball right where you are.

Learning your pattern

  • Why do I keep relapsing?A slip starts hours earlier. Catch an early link.
  • Why do I watch porn when I am stressed, bored or lonely?Often it is relief from a feeling. Meet the feeling another way.
  • How do I quit porn without relying on willpower?Fewer fights, not more willpower.

Bigger questions

  • Am I addicted to porn?Help does not wait for a label.
  • How long does it take to quit porn?Weeks, not days, and not a straight line.
  • Should I tell someone I am trying to quit porn?One safe person can share the weight.
