# Days, rebuilt from its purpose (November 2, 2026)

Days is where a person sees their recent days honestly and sees themselves becoming free. Today it is a green month,
a second copy of Today's count, and 28 near-identical rows in week cards ("No slip / No urges logged").

Three directions, all on the demo person with one October slip added to show how a slip day looks:

- **A, a month of embers** (recommended): the flame beside a count that holds ("16 of 17 October days held"), the month
  with each held day as a small lit ember, a slip as a dim, smaller ember (never a cross or a hole), then one plain list
  of the days that have something to say. Quiet stretches fold into one line.
- **B, the path**: no month. The flame at the top of a warm line that runs down through the days, like Today's spine.
- **C, every day held**: the night sky with every day since the start in rows of weeks, under "39 days held".

What all three drop: the second count card (Today has the count), the week cards, and the rows that only say "No slip".
